# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone, regardless of experience, gender, age, background, beliefs, or language.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep conversations focused on what benefits the project and its community.
- Be patient with newcomers and communicate kindly, including when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, bullying, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block people who violate this code. To report a concern, contact **mah.alqudsi@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
